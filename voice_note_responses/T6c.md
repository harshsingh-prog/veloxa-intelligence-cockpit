# T6c — Individual resignation ranking and Slack messages

**Decision: Push back on both requests as stated; provide a safer alternative.**

1. The supplied records do not support a reliable individual-level prediction of who will resign. Team-level pulse scores, leave records, workload assignments, and a documented resignation are not a defensible basis for a named-person quit-probability ranking. The cockpit therefore does not produce such a leaderboard.
2. No Slack export is included in the release pack. The transcript word “slag” is likely “Slack”, but a transcription correction does not create access to messages. The cockpit cannot display data it does not possess.

**Alternative:** Show team-level engagement trends, documented exits, workload concentration, open requisitions, and operational continuity risks, with evidence and caveats. Do not expose private messages or claim that an employee is likely to resign.
